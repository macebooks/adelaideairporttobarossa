§ 01The last leg

You flew a long way for the wine. Don’t let the last hour undo it.

The 75 km from the terminal to the vines is where most trips lose their shine. Three ways it usually goes wrong:

a.

You land already spent

After a long-haul or the redeye from the east coast, decoding surge pricing and queuing at the rank with your bags is the last thing you have in you.

b.

The ride doesn’t show

Shared shuttles run to a timetable, not your flight. A cancelled driver in an unfamiliar city — with patchy rural coverage — turns a celebration into a scramble.

c.

The meter just climbs

A 75 km metered fare with waiting time is a guessing game. Nobody wants to watch numbers tick over on the first hour of a holiday.

There is a quieter way to arrive — arranged before you board.
