Good to know

The questions people actually ask.

My flight's delayed — do I lose my booking?

No. We track your flight by its number, so if you land early or late your driver adjusts to the real arrival time. You don't need to call us from the plane.

Where exactly do I meet the driver?

Inside the Adelaide Airport arrivals hall. Your driver waits with a name sign after you clear baggage. Full meeting details come with your confirmation.

How much luggage can I bring?

Room for standard bags for every passenger, plus space for wine cases on the way back. Travelling with extra gear (golf clubs, prams, oversized cases)? Note it on the booking so we bring the right car.

Can you fit a child seat?

Yes, on request. Tell us the ages on your booking and we'll fit the right seat before pick-up at no guesswork to you.
