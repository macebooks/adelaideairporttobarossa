The arrival problem

The last hour of your journey shouldn't be the worst.

You've flown across the country — or the world — to taste some of Australia's finest wine. The transfer from ADL to the Barossa is where most trips lose their shine.

Jet-lagged & done

After a long-haul or interstate flight, the last thing you want is to decode ride-share surge pricing or wait in a taxi rank with your luggage.

Unreliable rides

Shared shuttles run to a timetable, not your flight. A cancelled driver in an unfamiliar city, with no rural coverage, turns a dream trip into stress.

Meter anxiety

A 75km metered fare with tolls and waiting time is a guessing game. You shouldn't have to watch a meter climb on your holiday.

There's a calmer way to reach the vines.

The private-transfer difference

One driver, one fixed fare, door to cellar door.

We're not a shared shuttle and not a taxi meter. We're a dedicated private chauffeur service built around one route we know intimately — ADL to the Barossa and back.

Flight-tracked meet & greet

We monitor your inbound flight in real time. Land early or late, your chauffeur is waiting at arrivals with a name board — no re-booking, no surge, no rush fees.

Fixed, flat pricing

You know the total before you book — one flat rate per vehicle, GST included. No meters, no tolls line-item, no per-bag charges, no holiday surge.

Private & door to door

Just you and your party — picked up at the terminal, dropped at your winery, hotel, or B&B doorstep. Child seats and extra luggage sorted on request.

Local driver-guides

Turn your transfer into a day. Our Barossa-based drivers know the boutique cellar doors, the long-lunch spots, and the roads the maps miss.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

What happens if my flight is delayed or early?
We track your flight in real time using your flight number, so your chauffeur adjusts automatically. Domestic arrivals include complimentary wait time and international arrivals include extra time for immigration and baggage — you're never charged a surge or rush fee for a delay outside your control. [Confirm exact free-wait windows with the owner.]
How much luggage can you carry?
The Executive Sedan comfortably fits up to 4 guests with 3–4 standard cases. For larger groups, extra luggage, wine cases, prams or golf clubs, choose the Premium People-Mover. Just tell us what you're carrying when you book and we'll match the right vehicle.
Do you provide child seats and capsules?
Yes — approved child seats, boosters and baby capsules are available on request at no surprise cost. Please note the ages and number of children when booking so we can fit the correct restraints in advance.
How large a group can you take?
Up to 4 in the sedan and up to 7 in the people-mover. For weddings, corporate incentives or wine-club groups of 8+, we arrange multiple vehicles on one coordinated flat quote. Ask us about group and return-trip rates.
