# Client information request — adelaideairporttobarossa.com.au

Send this to the business owner. Every item below is a launch blocker in `DEFINITION-OF-DONE.md`
(Tier 3). The site is built and tested; these are the last things only you can answer.

---

**Subject:** Last details needed before adelaideairporttobarossa.com.au goes live

Hi,

The new site is built and tested. Everything technical is done. There are a handful of things
only you can give me, and the site can't go live until I have them. You can reply straight down
this list.

**1. Your fares**

The site currently shows an estimator quoting these one-way prices, per car, Adelaide Airport to
the Barossa:

- Private sedan (up to 3 passengers): **$175**
- SUV (up to 4): **$215**
- Van (up to 7): **$275**

Please confirm these are your real, current prices, or send me the correct ones. I also need:

- **Return fare** — what do you charge for the round trip (airport → Barossa → airport)? Is there
  a discount versus booking two one-way transfers?
- **Day-tour rate** — your hourly rate and/or full-day rate for keeping the car for cellar doors.
- Do prices change by drop-off town (Lyndoch is closer, Angaston further), or is it one flat price
  across the valley?
- Anything extra that gets charged: after-hours or public holiday loading, child seats, extra
  stops, card surcharge.

**2. The vehicle**

- Make and model of the car you use for transfers (e.g. "Toyota Camry", "Kia Carnival").
- How many passengers it seats.
- If you run more than one vehicle, the same for each.

**3. Waiting time**

- How long do you wait free of charge after a flight lands before waiting time is charged?
- What do you charge per hour after that?

**4. Cancellations**

I've written the policy as: free cancellation more than 48 hours before pick-up, and inside 48
hours a fee may apply. Confirm that's right, and tell me:

- The exact cancellation fee inside 48 hours (a dollar amount or a percentage of the fare).
- What you charge for a no-show (nobody at the pick-up point, no contact).

**5. Customer reviews**

The site currently shows three guest quotes that I wrote as placeholders while designing the page.
They are not real reviews and cannot stay there when the site goes live — publishing invented
testimonials breaches the ACCC's rules on false or misleading testimonials, and it's the kind of
thing a competitor can report.

Please send me **three real reviews** with:

- the guest's words, as they wrote them;
- a first name and last initial;
- the trip type and destination (e.g. "Airport pick-up, Tanunda");
- confirmation that the guest is happy to be quoted on the website.

Google reviews are perfect for this. If you don't have three yet, tell me and I'll remove the
section until you do.

**6. Booking email**

The booking form emails you and sends the customer an automatic confirmation. To switch it on I
need:

- Confirmation that booking notifications should go to **barossacabs@outlook.com.au** (and any
  other addresses you want copied in).
- Access to your SMTP2GO account, or for you to create the sending key and send it to me
  privately (not by email — text or a password manager link).

Once it's live I'll send a test booking and you tell me it arrived.

**7. Domain and Google**

- Access to wherever `adelaideairporttobarossa.com.au` is registered, so I can point it at the
  site.
- Your **street address** as it appears on your Google Business Profile, and the link to that
  profile. Google uses this to rank you locally, so it materially affects how often you show up
  in Barossa transfer searches.
- Your ABN and registered business name, for the terms page.

**8. Tracking**

There is no analytics on the site right now, and the privacy policy says so. Do you want Google
Analytics installed so you can see how many people visit and book? If yes, I'll add it and update
the privacy policy to match.

Reply with whatever you have and I'll put it in. Items 1 to 5 are what's actually holding the
launch up.

Thanks,
