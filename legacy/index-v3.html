Station 01 — the crossing

The Barossa is 75 km from the plane. The hard part is the first 500 metres.

You're off a red-eye with a carousel full of bags and a phone on 4%. That's exactly the moment a rideshare surge or an empty taxi rank ruins the start of a trip you planned for months.

What usually goes sideways

  • Rideshare won't do the run, or quotes a fare that moves while you watch it.
  • The taxi rank is 40-deep and no one's heading 75 km out of the city.
  • The hire car looked cheap until the tolls, the fuel, and driving tired on an unfamiliar highway.
  • Three cases, four people, a case of wine to bring home — and no boot big enough.

How the crossing should feel

You walk out of customs. Someone's holding a board with your name on it. They take the heavy bag, you get in a clean, quiet car, and the next thing that matters is whether you start at Seppeltsfield or Tanunda. The fare was agreed before you left home.

  • No app, no surge, no meter climbing through Gawler.
  • One driver, gate to cellar door — not a relay of strangers.
  • Nap, take a call, or ask where the locals actually eat lunch.

Station 07 — the fine print

Before you book.

What if my flight is delayed or early?
We track your flight number from the moment you book. If you land early we're already there; if you're delayed, your driver adjusts and waits. Reasonable waiting time is built into the flat fare — you won't be charged for the airline running late.
Where exactly do we meet you?
Inside the Adelaide Airport arrivals hall, with a name-board, after you clear the baggage carousel (and customs on international arrivals). We'll send the exact meeting-point details and the driver's mobile the day before.
Is it really one flat fare — no meter?
Yes. You get a single all-in price in writing before you travel. No meter running through traffic, no per-bag charges, no after-hours or airport-pickup premium added at the end. What you're quoted is what you pay.
Can you carry luggage, wine and a child seat?
All of it. Tell us your case count, whether you're bringing wine home, and the ages of any children — we'll send a vehicle with the boot space and fit a baby capsule or booster at no drama. Larger groups get a people-mover.

Do you go to other regions, or only the Barossa?
The Barossa run is our specialty, but we also cover nearby cellar-door country and can arrange return transfers back to Adelaide Airport. Ask in your request and we'll confirm whether your destination is covered.
